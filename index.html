The Land of Grub Read Ready Initiative

Free sight word flashcards, built for how kids actually learn to read.

Just like good nutrition, reading is something that grows one small, repeated bite at a time. Reading proficiency by the end of third grade is one of the strongest known predictors of a child's long-term academic success — and most of that foundation is built on sight words, the common words (the, said, was, because) that kids need to recognize instantly rather than sound out letter by letter.

The Read Ready Initiative is our free, web-based sight word flashcard tool — made for families to use together at home, in the car, or anywhere a few spare minutes turn into reading practice. No login, no cost, no app store. Just open it and start.

Why flashcards actually work

  • Active recall, not passive exposure. Research shows the benefit comes from a child producing the word themselves — not just seeing it — so this tool always asks for a response.
  • It adapts to your child. Words they know fade into the background; words they're still learning come back around more often, automatically.
  • Words in context, too. Beyond the flashcard itself, each word comes with an example sentence — because isolated recognition and real reading comprehension are both part of the picture.
  • Most words take just a few repeats. Most readers commit a new sight word to memory in one to four exposures — so a few minutes, a few times a week, adds up faster than it feels like it should.
